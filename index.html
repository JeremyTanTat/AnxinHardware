2007Today

Anxin Hardware Trading Pte. Ltd. began as YCK Hardware Trading, with a simple aim: make it easier for customers to find the hardware they need.

Since then, we've grown through close partnerships with local dealers and customers across different professions. We source products from China, Taiwan, Malaysia, Singapore, Japan, Germany and beyond—bringing a broad selection together with quality assurance and competitive prices.
